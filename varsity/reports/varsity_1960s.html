Varsity reports, 1960s

Historical material from the Club archive.

On this page

Archive record. Original historical or technical material is retained here for reference.

Historical material is retained as part of the Club archive. Contemporary summaries may be edited, but original records and results are preserved.

Chris Williams has kindly provided the following results for us. The '62-'65 results were found in the CA Gazette. The '67 result was found in The Times archives.
