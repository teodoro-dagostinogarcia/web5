Half Blues

OUACC players who have received University Blues recognition.

Croquet is a Half Blue sport in Oxford. Half Blues are currently awarded to players who play off a handicap of 8 ‡ or lower at the annual Varsity match, although these criteria have been different in the past. Here we aim to maintain a record of all Half Blues awarded for croquet. If you received a Half Blue and are not on the list, please let us know.
